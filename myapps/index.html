PasteZen New

Copy anything. Paste it clean.

Use it toPaste clean text, without odd fonts, tracking links, or broken lines.

About

PasteZen lives in the menu bar and cleans what you copy at the moment you copy it. Fonts and colours are dropped, tracking parameters like utm_, fbclid, and gclid are removed from links, and broken PDF line breaks are joined again with Apple’s own on-device language tools. Password managers are detected and left alone, and the original text is always one click away.

Good for

  • Paste text without fonts and colours
  • Strip utm_, fbclid, and gclid from links
  • Fix broken lines in text copied from PDFs
  • Hide API keys and card numbers before I paste

Platforms & price

🖥 macOS Free + Pro upgrade 📋 Clean copy & paste

Key features

🧼 Plain Text on CopyFonts, colours, and hidden formatting are removed before the text reaches your clipboard.
🔗 Clean Links (Pro)Removes utm_, fbclid, gclid, and similar tracking parameters from every link you copy.
📄 PDF Line Repair (Pro)Column breaks and hyphens are joined again on your Mac, using Apple’s language tools.
🕶 Smart Redaction (Pro)Masks 13 kinds of secrets, including API keys and card numbers, before you paste them.
↩️ One-Click RestoreThe original text is kept, so you can get it back any time.

Draw on your screen. Copy it instantly.

Use it toDraw on your screen during calls, reviews, and bug reports.
