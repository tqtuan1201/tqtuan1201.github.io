Motivation

I got tired of two things in my daily workflow on macOS:

  1. Leaving my Mac for a coffee and wondering if someone would touch or inspect it.
  2. Running long builds, downloads, or reading documentation, only to come back and find my Mac asleep.

Instead of complaining, I spent the last few months building a solution for myself and the developer community.

Today, I’m excited to launch Proximity Lock — a native security and productivity companion built for the Apple ecosystem.

What It Does

Proximity Lock seamlessly bridges your iPhone and Mac to deliver continuous security and convenience:

  • Auto-Lock / Unlock: Your Mac locks and unlocks automatically based on your iPhone’s proximity.
  • Cafe Mode: If someone touches your Mac while you’re away, a siren sounds and a photo is sent to your iPhone.
  • Work Mode: Keeps your Mac awake during long builds, downloads, or research while the security layer stays active.
  • Remote Control: Lock or put your Mac to sleep from your iPhone with a single tap.
